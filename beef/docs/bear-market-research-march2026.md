- **Источники:** [FBI IC3](https://www.ic3.gov/psa/2025/psa250226), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/03/04/north-korea-bybit-hack-ethereum-safe-dprk-lazarus-group-tradertraitor/)
