## Key Findings

### 1. Persona Divergence is Real
The gap between highest and lowest persona score per roast averaged **3.4 points** (out of 10). This validates the multi-persona approach — single-evaluator scoring would miss audience-specific dynamics.

Largest divergence: **#18 (OpenSea "ser")** — Meme Lord 8.0 vs Builder 2.0 (Δ6.0). The same roast is genius to one segment and noise to another.

### 2. The #1 Surprise: #7 (Virtuals AI Solidarity)
Self-eval ranked it 3rd (38/40). Persona panel ranked it **1st** (composite 0.820). The first-person AI narrator voice unlocked engagement from segments that otherwise scored low:
- Meme Lord: 7.5 (his 2nd highest — the format surprised him)
- AI Enthusiast: 8.75 (his highest by far — "literal catnip")
- The self-aware AI angle is the bot's unique weapon

### 3. The #1 Disappointment: #11 (Hyperliquid "FTX-shaped")
Self-eval gave it 39/40 (tied #1). Persona panel dropped it to **7th** (composite 0.764). Why:
- Meme Lord: 4.0 ("reads as a summary tweet, no comedic payoff")
- Newbie: 3.5 ("FTX-shaped requires context she lacks")
- High L2 score ≠ high audience engagement — this validates the need for Layer 3

### 4. "this is fine" and "few understand" Are Dead Formats
Both #6 ("this is fine") and #3 ("few understand") were penalized by the Meme Lord and Builder for using borrowed CT sign-offs. The Meme Lord explicitly flagged: "pasted onto 800 CT posts this year."

**Actionable:** Remove "this is fine," "few understand," "probably nothing" from the prompt's encouraged phrases. They signal "this was generated by someone who read the CT handbook."

### 5. L1 Brevity Score Doesn't Predict L3 Success
#18 scored highest on L1 (27/30, thanks to brevity bonus) but 8th on composite (0.748). Short ≠ shareable for all segments. Builder ignored it (2.0), Analyst found it too thin (4.25). Brevity is a bonus only when the insight is already loaded from context.

### 6. Segment-Specific Content Strategy Emerged

| Content type | Best audience | Worst audience | Production note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-aware AI narrator | Meme Lord + AI Enthusiast + Degen | Builder (neutral) | Use on AI/agent targets — unique $BEEF weapon |
| Structural thesis ("infrastructure eats builders") | Builder + Analyst | Newbie | Use when targeting ecosystem dynamics |
| Universal metaphor ("ghost leaving haunted house") | Newbie + Analyst + Degen | Meme Lord (seen it) | Use on dead projects — accessibility > edge |
| One-liner under 100 chars | Meme Lord + Newbie | Builder + Analyst | Use for timely events where context is pre-loaded |
| Data-heavy arc with punchline | Degen + Analyst | Newbie + Meme Lord | Default format — reliable but not peak |
